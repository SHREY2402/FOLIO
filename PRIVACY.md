# Folio Privacy Notice

**Last updated:** 1 November 2026

Folio is a browser-based document scanner and file creation tool. Its scanning, image, PDF and spreadsheet creation tools can be used without an account.

## Scans and files

Your documents, images and locally created files are saved in the **IndexedDB storage of the browser and device you use**. Folio does not automatically send those files to the app owner, GitHub or Google. Files leave your device when you choose an export, sharing destination or upload action. Browser storage can be cleared or deleted, so keep exported backups of important documents.

## Google sign-in (optional)

When the owner activates Firebase Authentication, signing in with Google may provide the app with your Google display name, email address, profile picture and an account identifier. Firebase Authentication and Google process sign-in data according to their terms and privacy notices. Signing in does not sync or back up your documents.

## Premium payments

Payments are **disabled**. Pricing shown in Folio is a planned offering only. If payments are activated later, the notice will be updated to explain the payment processor and any account records needed for purchases.

## Tracking and advertising

The current Folio build has no third-party advertising or dedicated analytics tracker. Firebase authentication requests (if enabled) and standard GitHub Pages hosting requests are processed by their respective providers.

## Removing data

You can delete scans in Folio's library. Clearing your browser's site data deletes locally saved Folio files. If you sign in, Firebase Authentication account information exists separately from local scans. For questions about the open-source project, use GitHub Issues at https://github.com/SHREY2402/FOLIO/issues.

This notice should be reviewed by the project owner before a production launch and updated whenever data handling changes.
